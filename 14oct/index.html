Quantum University, Roorkee

About Quantum University

Quantum University, Roorkee is a private university in Uttarakhand offering undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs with an interdisciplinary approach, modern infrastructure and industry-oriented education.

Programs Offered

B.Tech, M.Tech, BCA, MCA, MBA, BBA, B.Com, B.Sc, Pharmacy, Agriculture, Law, Hotel Management and many other undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

Campus Facilities

Smart classrooms, advanced laboratories, central library, hostel, sports facilities, Wi-Fi campus, innovation labs and placement assistance for students.
